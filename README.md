# Apex Engineering Search

Act as a premier UI/UX engineer specializing in ultra-premium, dark-mode digital experiences for luxury automotive and elite engineering brands. Build a single-page corporate website mockup for "Crowden International Search".

Visual Identity & Background Styling:

- Background Material: Create a subtle, dark, repeating matte carbon-fibre texture across the layout. 

- Aerodynamic Lines: Overlay thin, glowing, or sharp geometric vector lines that flow across the sections to mimic aerodynamic wind tunnel streams, air currents, and precision engineering schematics.

- Color Palette: Deep charcoal, matte black, stark white for text, and an electric silver or racing red accent for key elements.

Typography & Fonts:

- Do NOT use generic sans-serif fonts. 

- Change the primary headers to a high-end, wide, futuristic tech font like 'Orbitron', 'Syne', or a sharp geometric typeface (e.g., 'Space Grotesk' or 'Clash Display') to look premium, modern, and engineering-focused.

- Use a highly clean, legible, hyper-modern sans-serif (like 'Inter' or 'Plus Jakarta Sans') for the body copy to maintain boardroom legibility.

Sections to Generate:

1. HERO SECTION: A bold, premium headline matching elite F1/Aerospace resourcing. Feature the custom typography prominently. Background shows aerodynamic wind-tunnel line vectors tracing across a dark carbon-fibre background. Two clean CTAs: "Retain Our Firm" and "Secure Candidate Portal".

2. SECTOR FOCUS (Bento Grid): Sharp, bordered bento blocks detailing their core industries: Formula 1 & Motorsport, Aerospace Engineering, and Luxury Automotive. 

3. THE SECURE GATEWAY: A central dashboard UI element showing an "Intelligent Talent Mapping" system. Text should explain how candidates can securely submit credentials to a private, walled AI infrastructure that maps complex engineering skills. Frame the container to look like a high-security digital vault.

4. METRICS OF SUCCESS: Clean, oversized typographic numbers (e.g., Global Placements, Confidentiality Rate) separated by thin, elegant lines.

Copy Tone:

Sophisticated, peer-to-peer enterprise English. Use industry-specific vocabulary like "Aerodynamic Acumen", "Discrete Technical Sourcing", "Transferable Engineering Expertise", and "Boardroom Authority".
